Bastiat and Mill

Two economists writing at the same moment, the late 1840s, who took the same inheritance in opposite directions. Bastiat pushed utilitarianism to its logical end; Mill tried one last time to hold the utility and labor perspectives together.

Game 1: Standing on Shoulders. For each idea, you’ll see what an earlier thinker said, then decide what Bastiat or Mill did with it: kept it, extended it, changed it, or rejected it. Page numbers in parentheses refer to Hunt & Lautzenheiser, Chapter 8.
Content based on Chapter 8 (“Pure Versus Eclectic Utilitarianism: The Writings of Bastiat and Mill,” pp. 174–200) of Hunt & Lautzenheiser, History of Economic Thought.
