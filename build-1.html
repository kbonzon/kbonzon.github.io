Build 1

Pre–Adam Smith and Adam Smith

Two role-playing games: step into the lives of the people of each era and make the choices they would have made.

Note: I have chosen to study Adam Smith and the thinkers before him, since we have not talked much about Malthus yet. I will wait for the study guide to decide whether I need to make a game to help me with Malthus.

How to play: pick a game, then pick a role. Each role has a few tasks. Choose what that person would really have done at the time, then read why. Your progress is saved in this browser.

Game content based on Chapters 2 and 3 of the course textbook, History of Economic Thought.
