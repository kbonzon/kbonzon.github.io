Bentham, Say, and Senior

Three writers who moved economics from the labor theory of value toward utility and social harmony. Work through each one’s ideas, then test where they agree and where they split.

The course question: does market exchange harmonize our interests, or conceal a conflict between them? Keep it in mind for each writer. Page numbers in parentheses refer to Hunt & Lautzenheiser, Chapter 6.
Content based on Chapter 6 (“Rationalistic Subjectivism: The Economics of Bentham, Say, and Senior,” pp. 125–151) of Hunt & Lautzenheiser, History of Economic Thought.
