David Ricardo’s Model

A hands-on workbook. Each lesson gives you the numbers, draws the figure, and walks you through the model one calculation at a time.

How it works: type your answer and press Check. If you’re stuck, a hint appears, and after two tries you can reveal the answer. Some steps let you pick new numbers or drag sliders to see the model move. Your scores are saved in this browser.
Workbook content based on Chapter 5 (“David Ricardo”) of the course textbook, History of Economic Thought.
